Policy Document for U.S. Employees
Tuition Assistance | Degree Programs | Certificates | Bootcamps | Language Learning
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
​

JPMorgan Chase partners with Guild Education to provide comprehensive educational benefits that remove financial barriers to continued learning and career advancement. This policy document outlines the firm's Guild Education benefit program effective January 1, 2025, including 100% tuition coverage for select undergraduate and master's degree programs, up to $7,500 annually for other graduate programs and certificates, up to $5,250 annually for other undergraduate programs, tuition reimbursement for approved programs outside the Guild catalog, and access to personal coaching and career guidance.
​

With over 500 programs from more than 20 accredited institutions, the Guild Education benefit reflects JPMorgan Chase's commitment to investing in employee development, providing debt-free education opportunities, and building a future-ready workforce. JPMorgan Chase believes that education is a cornerstone of opportunity and career growth and provides employees with access to high-quality academic programs, financial support, and expert guidance to help achieve educational and career goals.
​

JPMorgan Chase Guild Education Benefit – Executive Summary
Guild Education is a public benefit corporation that helps major employers deliver tuition assistance programs to their employees. Guild partners with companies including JPMorgan Chase, Target, Disney, Chipotle, Lowe's, Walmart, and others to manage education benefits efficiently.

Curated catalog of 500+ learning programs from over 20 schools and universities

Section 2: Eligibility Requirements
2.1 Employee Eligibility
To be eligible for Guild Education benefits, employees must:
​

Be actively employed by JPMorgan Chase on a U.S. payroll

Be benefits-eligible (regularly scheduled to work 20 or more hours per week)

Not be classified as temporary, seasonal, or contingent worker

Maintain active employment throughout program enrollment

Employment status:

Full-time employees: Eligible

Part-time employees (20+ hours/week): Eligible

Temporary, seasonal, contingent workers: Not eligible

Employees on approved leave: May remain eligible; consult Human Resources
​

No minimum service requirement:

Benefits are available immediately upon achieving benefits-eligible status; there is no waiting period or minimum length of service.
​

2.2 Academic Eligibility
Programs must:
​

Be listed in the Guild catalog or approved for tuition reimbursement

Be offered by schools accredited by recognized accrediting bodies

Meet quality standards established by Guild and JPMorgan Chase

Maintain academic standing to continue funding
​

Enrollment status:

Part-time or full-time enrollment accepted

Online, hybrid, or campus-based programs eligible

Programs designed for working adults with flexible schedules

Section 5: Tuition Reimbursement for Non-Guild Programs
5.1 Tuition Reimbursement Overview
For approved programs outside the Guild catalog, JPMorgan Chase offers tuition reimbursement:
​

Undergraduate programs: Up to $5,250 per year

Master’s degrees: Up to $7,500 per year

Certificates and certifications: Up to $7,500 per year

Employee pays tuition upfront and, upon successful completion, submits a reimbursement request; JPMorgan Chase reimburses up to the annual cap.
​

5.2 Eligibility for Tuition Reimbursement
Programs must be:

Offered by an accredited institution recognized by the U.S. Department of Education

Relevant to the employee's current role or career development at JPMorgan Chase

Pre-approved by employee's manager and HR

Academic degree, certificate, or certification programs (not leisure or hobby courses)
​

Pre-approval process:

Employee identifies program and institution.

Employee discusses program with manager to confirm career relevance.

Employee submits pre-approval request via Guild platform or HR system.

Manager and HR review and approve or deny request.

Employee receives written approval before enrolling.
​

5.3 Reimbursement Procedures
Step-by-step process:

Pre-approval: Obtain written approval.

Enrollment: Enroll and pay tuition.

Course completion: Successfully complete course with minimum required grade.

Documentation: Gather receipts, transcripts, proof of payment.

Submission: Submit reimbursement request.

Review: HR reviews for completeness and compliance.

Reimbursement: Paid via payroll or direct deposit.
​

Required documentation:

Official grade report or transcript

Proof of tuition payment

Course syllabus or description

Pre-approval confirmation
​

Minimum grade requirements:

Undergraduate: Grade of C or higher (2.0/4.0)

Graduate: Grade of B or higher (3.0/4.0)

Pass/Fail: Passing grade required

Certifications: Successful exam completion or credential attainment
​

Reimbursement timeline:

Submit within 90 days of course completion.

HR processes within 30 days.

Reimbursement included in next payroll cycle after approval.
​

5.4 Annual Caps and Limitations
Caps are based on calendar year (January 1–December 31).

Unused amounts do not carry over.

Employees may combine Guild catalog funding and reimbursement up to annual caps.
​

Tax implications:

IRS allows up to $5,250 per year in tax-free employer educational assistance.

Amounts exceeding $5,250 are taxable income and reported on W-2.

Section 10: Tax Implications
10.1 Tax-Free Educational Assistance
Under IRS Section 127, employer-provided educational assistance is tax-free up to $5,250 per calendar year.
​

Tax-free benefits include:

Tuition

Fees

Books

Supplies

Equipment
​

10.2 Taxable Educational Assistance
The first $5,250 of educational assistance per year is excluded from gross income.

Amounts above $5,250 are taxable income, reported on W-2 and subject to federal, Social Security, and Medicare taxes.
​

Example:

Employee receives $7,500 in tuition assistance.

$5,250 is tax-free; $2,250 is taxable income.
​

10.3 Tax Deductions and Credits
Employees may qualify for:
​

Lifetime Learning Credit (up to $2,000 per return)

American Opportunity Tax Credit (up to $2,500 per eligible student)

Tax credits are based on qualified expenses not covered by tax-free employer assistance, and employees should consult a tax advisor and IRS Publication 970.
​

10.4 Tax Reporting
Educational assistance up to $5,250 is not included in Box 1 wages.

Assistance exceeding $5,250 is included in Box 1 wages.
​

Employees must review their W-2 and report educational assistance correctly, and may wish to consult a tax professional.
