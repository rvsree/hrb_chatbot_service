Chunking Strategy

Splitting one document's extracted text into appropriately-sized chunks before embedding - 7 real strategies (text_chunker.py), auto-selected by content shape or chosen explicitly per document.

Overview Spec Design Decisions
1
Intent

Every uploaded document gets chunked before its pieces are embedded and indexed - how those pieces are drawn affects retrieval quality directly (too small loses context, too large dilutes an embedding across unrelated ideas). This feature picks a reasonable default automatically from the document's own shape, while still letting HR Support override it per document from the Documents page when the default guess isn't right.

2
Scope
Live todaybuilt
  • 7 real strategies: fixed, recursive, semantic, markdown, html, none, document_structure
  • Auto-selection (decide_chunking_strategy()) from markdown/HTML heading sniffing, falling back to document length
  • Auto-scaled chunk size for long documents (>10k chars gets 1500 instead of the 1000 default)
  • An oversized-chunk safety net every strategy respects, not just the 3 that take size as a direct split target
  • Tables pulled out before any strategy runs, kept as their own whole chunk regardless of strategy
  • Per-document re-chunk from the Documents page UI - re-embeds and re-indexes with the newly chosen strategy
Out of scopeby design
  • semantic and document_structure are explicit-only - never auto-selected
  • Embedding generation and the actual vector-store write - this feature's output is just text chunks
  • No chunk-quality scoring or A/B comparison between strategies - the choice is manual/heuristic, not measured
3
Personas

hr_support - uploads documents (auto-chunked) and can explicitly re-chunk an already-indexed one from the Documents page's "Chunking Strategy" control, same role gate as document upload itself.

4
Acceptance criteria
A table is never split across chunksmet

Every [TABLE]...[/TABLE] block is pulled out before chunking and reattached whole afterward, regardless of strategy.

No chunk exceeds the resolved sizemet

_split_oversized_chunks() re-splits anything over the cap, after any of the 7 strategies runs.

Auto-selection never silently picks an explicit-only strategymet

semantic/document_structure can only be reached by a caller explicitly naming them.

Re-chunking a document actually changes its retrievable contentmet

The rechunk route re-embeds and re-indexes with the new strategy, not just relabeling existing chunks.
